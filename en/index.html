01 · About FOMA

A museum that collects how cars are born

From the Pony to the Santa Fe — sketches, drawings, clay models and master bucks gathered over 35 years by Park Jong-seo, a first-generation Korean car designer. FOMA is Korea's first automobile design museum, collecting the forgotten story of process rather than the finished result.
